## LinkedIn — Featured / Projects description (≈ 150 words)

**AI in HR: The Reality Check**

How much of what we hear about AI in hiring holds up? I built an interactive, evidence-based dashboard to find out.

It compares three things that are rarely put side by side: what vendors and industry reports claim, what adoption surveys actually measure, and how employers experience recruitment technology in practice. The last builds on my MSc HRM dissertation at the University of Sussex, *Beyond Adoption*. It found that small hospitality employers often meet ranking and filtering inside ordinary job boards without thinking of them as "AI", and that withdrawal or deliberate non-use can be reasoned choices.

Inside: a claims-vs-evidence explorer with transparent assessments, adoption dashboards that refuse to pool incompatible statistics, a UK/EU governance and risk explorer with a procurement checklist, and a searchable library of 74 verified sources.

Built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind and Recharts. https://ai-hr-reality-check.vercel.app

## LinkedIn post (optional, shorter)

"31% of UK organisations use AI in recruitment." That figure is real (CIPD, 2024). But a new "don't know" option was added that year, it is weighted by employment, and it only counts AI that HR teams recognise as AI.

I built **AI in HR: The Reality Check** to show what headline numbers like this include and what they leave out. It brings together adoption data, independent research on bias, efficiency and candidate quality, UK and EU regulation, and my MSc research on how small employers actually encounter hiring algorithms.
